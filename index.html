Settings ⚙️

Privacy 🛡️

LOOMA local privacy

This single-file prototype stores settings, saved ideas, posts, closet items and other app data in your browser's local storage.

Camera access is requested only when you press the camera button. Photos taken in the Photobooth are processed in your browser.

This prototype does not provide a real cloud account system. Real online accounts, cloud storage and synchronized messaging require a backend.

Terms 📄

LOOMA prototype terms

LOOMA is currently a front-end prototype. Features that appear to simulate accounts, premium access or messaging do not automatically create real online services.

Do not upload material you do not have permission to use.

Actual paid subscriptions must be connected to a legitimate payment provider before users are charged.
